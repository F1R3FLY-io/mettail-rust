================================================================
INTERPRETATION
================================================================

1. The actual edge_stack_arena ALREADY dedups about as well as a coarse
   EdgeKind keying would. There is no per-arena structural reduction
   available at the (parent, EdgeKind) granularity.

2. The plan's prediction of 450,000x ratio assumed cursors at the same
   chain depth would share an InfixContinuation history. Empirically
   they do NOT — cohort branching produces distinct EdgeKind sequences
   per cursor, so the projected_parent diverges even when the immediate
   kind matches.

3. The 225 M actual nodes for 29 M cursors observation (Exp 16 r3) is
   therefore NOT caused by GssEdgeId over-distinguishing semantically
   equivalent edges. It is caused by genuinely distinct cursor histories.

4. Per-call hit_ratio decreases monotonically with chain size on LEFT-
   assoc (0.22 → 0.15 → 0.13). Per-arena ratio stays at 1.00x because
   both arenas grow at the same rate.

5. RIGHT-assoc shows a small structural win (1.03x): a few projected
   nodes consolidate due to repeated exponentiation rules. Not
   significant.
